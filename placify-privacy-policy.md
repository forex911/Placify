# Privacy Policy

**Placify**
Last updated: [20 September 2026]

## 1. Who we are

Placify ("Placify", "we", "us") is a web application that helps students track their placement preparation, including job and internship applications, DSA progress, study tasks, notes, hackathons and LeetCode statistics. Placify is operated by forex911.

This policy explains what information Placify handles, why we handle it, who else may see it, and the choices you have. By using Placify you agree to the practices described here.

## 2. Information we handle

**Content you add to Placify.** This is the information you type in yourself:

- Applications: company name, role, status, deadline, and an optional link to the job posting.
- DSA tracker: topics and your progress on each.
- Study tasks: task titles, descriptions, dates and completion status.
- Notes: titles, text and any color you choose.
- Hackathons: event names, dates, project links and outcomes.

**LeetCode information.** If you link a LeetCode username, we send that username to LeetCode's public API to retrieve your solved-problem counts (total, easy, medium and hard). We store the username and the counts so they can appear on your dashboard.

**Account information.** We collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password during registration. You may also authenticate using third-party providers like Google.

**Technical information.** Like most web services, our servers and hosting provider may automatically record technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and timestamps. We use it to keep the service running, secure and debuggable.

**Preferences on your device.** Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is not sent to us.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information such as financial details, government ID numbers or health data. Please do not put that kind of information in your notes or other fields.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- provide and operate Placify's features, including your dashboard, statistics and deadline warnings;
- retrieve and display your LeetCode statistics;
- keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix problems;
- understand how the service is performing so we can improve it; and
- comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your content for advertising.

## 4. Who else may receive information

- **LeetCode.** When you link a handle, your username is sent to LeetCode to fetch your public statistics. LeetCode handles that request under its own terms and privacy policy. Placify is not affiliated with LeetCode.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers.** Placify's frontend is hosted on Vercel, and the backend and database are hosted on cloud providers such as Render, Railway, or Neon. They process data on our behalf to run the service.
- **Google Fonts.** Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers and your IP address is visible to Google for that request.
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety or security of users or the service.

Placify currently does not use third-party analytics or error-tracking providers.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

Placify uses your browser's local storage to remember your theme preference. We also use JSON Web Tokens (JWT) for secure authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## 6. How long we keep information

We keep your content for as long as you use Placify. If you ask us to delete your data, we will delete it from our live systems within 30 days. Backups may take up to 90 days to be overwritten. We may keep limited technical logs for security and troubleshooting for up to 90 days.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, including using HTTPS for all traffic, implementing JWT-based authentication, restricting database access, and using securely hashed passwords. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please choose strong passwords and keep them private.

## 8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

- ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
- correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- ask us to erase your information;
- withdraw your consent to processing (this may mean we can no longer provide the service to you);
- nominate another person to exercise your rights on your behalf if you are unable to; and
- raise a grievance about how we handle your information.

You can also edit or delete most of your content directly inside Placify at any time. To make a request, contact us at the address in Section 11.

## 9. Children

Placify is intended for users who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Where data is processed

Depending on where our hosting providers operate, your information may be processed on servers outside your home country. We choose providers that apply reasonable safeguards, and we handle information according to this policy wherever it is processed.
